Our Wild, Wacky, Wonderful Family Story

Meet the real people, kindred spirits, and family teamwork behind every uplifting story, illustration, and hug sent from our studio in New Smyrna Beach, Florida.

"Life Doesn't Need to Be Perfect to Be Wonderful"

I love my life. It's a wild, wacky, wonderful roller coaster full of fun, laughter, and sharp turns. I'm 67 years young, dance the Hula twice a week, eat way too much dark chocolate, ride my little red scooter around our funky beach town of New Smyrna Beach, and create art right across from the ocean.

"Life is all about how you handle Plan B. Real living begins where your comfort zone ends, and pretending to be a normal person day after day is exhausting!"

The Florida Ocean Studio

Every character, poem, and piece of whimsical lettering is born in our Florida studio. We are not a nameless corporation — every order is lovingly handled, packaged with care, and shipped out by our close-knit family team.

The People Who Make the Magic Happen

It truly takes a family to run this hurricane-in-a-teacup business!

"Big Al" Toronto

Husband of 39+ Years • Operations & Finance

Al is Suzy's rock and self-described "Beast of Burden" at craft festivals and art shows. While Suzy brings the wild creative tornado, Al provides the essential left-brain legal, logistical, and financial backbone that keeps the studio thriving. Without him, Suzy Toronto Studios simply wouldn't exist!

The practical rock behind the wacky dreams

Chase Toronto

Son • IT, Systems & Brand Originator

The youngest of five children, Chase is the wacky kid who started it all! As an innocent child, his question — "Mommy, who is she that grew me in her tummy?" — inspired the entire Wonderful Wacky Women line. Today, he's a computer genius and pilot who oversees business systems and tech.

The spark that ignited "Wonderful Wacky Women"

Robert "Rob" Thimm

Art Director for 20+ Years

Rob is literally the sticky, glittery glue stick holding the studio together. Serving as Art Director for over two decades, he translates Suzy's hurricane of creative sketches into finished cards, calendars, and books. He is family in every sense of the word.

The calm anchor of the creative studio

Launi

Best Friend & Kindred Spirit

Suzy's lifelong bestie who inspired famous pieces like Kindred Spirits and Gathering of the Goddesses. Together, Suzy and Launi have weathered life's greatest storms, proving that sisterhood and unconditional friendship are life's greatest blessings.

The inspiration for sacred sisterhood

The Next Generation

5 Children • 16 Grandkids • 1 Great-Grand

From assembling order boxes to testing product ideas, Suzy's sprawling family remains actively involved. Every purchase directly supports this multigenerational American family business and keeps the tradition of heartfelt, uplifting art alive.
